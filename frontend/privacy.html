2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you (OTP-based mobile login).
  • To run our eligibility assessment and generate a loan offer.
  • To enable our partner NBFC lenders and banks to verify your identity, assess creditworthiness, and disburse loans.
  • To communicate updates about your application, repayments, and support.
  • To detect fraud, maintain security, and comply with legal and regulatory obligations.
  • To improve our app, with aggregated, de-identified analytics.

3. How we share information

Because we are a Loan Service Provider (LSP), we act as a facilitator only. We share your information with our RBI-registered partner NBFCs and banks — the actual lenders — only to the extent required to provide your loan. We do not sell your personal data.

  • Partner lenders (NBFCs/banks): identity, financial details and documents needed for KYC, credit decision and disbursal.
  • Service providers: cloud hosting, SMS/email delivery, verification and analytics vendors acting on our behalf.
  • Legal/regulatory: when required by law, regulation or a valid court order.

5. Data security & retention

  • We use bank-grade encryption (TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256 at rest) and access controls.
  • We keep your personal data only as long as needed to provide services and meet legal retention requirements (typically up to 8 years for KYC records as per RBI norms), after which it is securely deleted or anonymised.
  • In the event of a data breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

6. Your rights & choices

  • Access & correction: review and update your profile information anytime in the app.
  • Delete account: request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to legal retention).
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw consent for optional processing at any time.
  • Opt out of marketing: unsubscribe from promotional communications.
  • Complaint: raise a grievance with our Grievance Officer (details below), and you may also escalate to the RBI's Integrated Ombudsman Scheme where applicable.

7. Cookies & analytics

Our website uses essential cookies for functionality and privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate usage. We do not use cookies for targeted advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

8. Children's privacy

11. Terms of use

Loan Service Provider (LSP) disclaimer

Financily Technologies Pvt. Ltd. is a Loan Service Provider (LSP) and digital lending marketplace. We are NOT a lender, an NBFC, or a bank, and we do NOT hold an NBFC licence. We do not lend money directly.

  • All loans are provided by our RBI-registered partner NBFCs and banks, who make the final credit decision, set the terms, and disburse funds.
  • We facilitate the loan application, eligibility assessment, and document collection on behalf of these partner lenders.
  • Loan approval, interest rates, amounts, tenure and repayment schedules are determined solely by the partner lender based on their policies and your credit profile.
  • Submitting an application does not guarantee loan approval.
  • Interest rates vary by product and are capped at 33% p.a. maximum. See the representative example below.
  • You must be 18+ years old, an Indian resident, and have a valid bank account and KYC documents to use the service.
  • This platform is provided for lawful personal loan facilitation only. It is not financial advice.

Repayment & default

  • Repayment is via monthly auto-debit from your bank account or manual payment through the app.
  • You may prepay fully or partially at any time; foreclosure is free after the first 3 EMIs on most products.
  • Late payment attracts a penalty as per the lender's policy, and may affect your credit score.

Acceptance

By accessing the Financily app or website, you agree to these Terms of Use and our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the service.
