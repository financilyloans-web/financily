Eligibility and documents

Requirements vary by loan type and lender. This is a general guide.

Basic eligibility

  • Indian resident, age 21 to 65 at maturity (varies by lender)
  • Steady salary or business income
  • Good credit history preferred
  • For secured loans: clear property or vehicle title

Common documents

  • Aadhaar, PAN, passport photo
  • Address proof
  • Last 6 months bank statement
  • Salary slips or ITR and business proof
  • Property or vehicle papers for secured loans

    Your CIBIL score decides your terms

    300 – 650

    Needs improvement

    Approval is harder and rates are higher. A co-applicant or collateral may be needed.

    • Pay every EMI and card bill on time
    • Keep card use under 30% of limit
    • Fix errors in your credit report
    650 – 750

    Good

    Fair chance of approval with moderate rates. Reaching 750 can unlock better offers.

    • Avoid many loan enquiries at once
    • Clear small dues before applying
    750 and above

    Excellent

    Strongest chance of approval, lower rates, higher limits and faster processing.

    • Best position to negotiate
    • Keep your habits steady
    300650750900
