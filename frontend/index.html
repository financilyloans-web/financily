Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Financily.

Am I eligible for a loan?
You need to be 18+ with a steady income and a valid ID. Most salaried and self-employed applicants are eligible. Use the Eligibility Checker above for a quick estimate.
What documents do I need?
Just your PAN, Aadhaar and a bank account. Everything is verified digitally via e-KYC — no printed documents, no couriers.
What are the interest rates?
Each product has its own starting rate: Personal Loan from 26% p.a. (salaried from 16%), Cash Loan from 29%, and Business Loan from 19%. The maximum interest rate is capped at 33% p.a. Your exact rate depends on your profile, amount and tenure — and is always shown before you accept the offer.
How do I repay the loan?
Auto-debit from your bank account works monthly EMI. You can also repay manually from the app anytime.
Can I prepay the loan early?
Yes — you can prepay fully or partially at any time with no hidden charges on most products. Foreclosure is free after 3 EMIs.
What if I miss an EMI?
A late fee applies, and we'll remind you before the due date. Contact us immediately if you're facing difficulty — we can restructure your plan.
Is my data safe?
Yes. We use bank-grade encryption, never sell your data, and are registered with the RBI. Your data is used only to process your loan.
